Read Input Events

read_events

Read the input events (clicks, scroll, cursor, shortcuts, typing bursts) of a recording made with events: true, in video seconds and pixels; e.g. types: ['down'], then sample_frames there.

captureread-onlyidempotentsince v0.28.0

00:00PARAMETERS
NameTypeDescription

types("down" | "up" | "move" | "wheel" | "key" | "typing" | "gap")[]down/up (mouse buttons; clicks are down), move (sampled cursor), wheel, key (shortcut), typing (burst), gap (events may be missing).
inFrameOnlybooleanDrop events whose cursor was outside the recorded area.

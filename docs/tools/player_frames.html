Player Frames

player_frames

Preview frames for the review player: JPEGs on an even grid over the video plus frames at scene changes, with their real times. Used by the inline player; agents should use sample_frames or contact_sheet instead.

watchread-onlyidempotentapp-onlysince v0.33.0

maxFramesintegerFrames on the even grid (default 120); scene-change frames come on top, up to as many again. ≥ 1, ≤ 300
widthintegerFrame width (default 640). ≥ 64, ≤ 1280

SCREENCAST-MCP / TOOLS / PRODUCE

Overlay Image or Video

overlay

Composite an image or video (logo, watermark, picture-in-picture) onto a video at a pixel position, optionally scaled and time-limited; keeps the base's audio and length.

producewritesdestructivesince v0.3.0

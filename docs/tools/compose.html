Compose Videos

compose

Lay 2-9 videos out side by side, stacked, or in a grid (before/after, app and terminal), labelled and in sync.

producewritesdestructivesince next release

00:00PARAMETERS
NameTypeDescription
inputs requiredobject[]Videos in reading order, each with an optional label (e.g. "Before"). at least 2 items, at most 9 items
inputs[].path requiredstring
inputs[].labelstring at most 80 characters
layout"side_by_side" | "stack" | "grid"side_by_side (default), stack, or grid.
alignMarkerstringLine inputs up on this add_marker label.
endWith"longest" | "shortest"longest (default; shorter ones hold their last frame) or shortest.
audiointeger | "mix" | "none"Input index whose audio to keep (default 0), "mix", or "none".
outputstringOutput path (default: under SCREENCAST_HOME/edits).
overwritebooleanReplace an existing output file (default false).
